## 1. Understanding the Internet

The internet is a worldwide collection of interconnected networks that allows devices to communicate and exchange information.

It is called a **network of networks** because it connects millions of individual networks around the world.

Nobody owns the entire internet. Different organisations manage their own networks and infrastructure.

### How Information Travels

Information can travel through different communication media:

- Telephone wires
- Fibre-optic cables
- Wireless transmissions
- Satellite links

## 2. Local Networks

A local network connects devices within a limited geographical area, such as a home, office, school or college.

### Small Home Network

A small home network connects personal devices such as:

- Smartphones
- Laptops
- Tablets
- Printers
- Smart TVs

It allows users to share resources and access the internet.

### SOHO Network

SOHO stands for **Small Office/Home Office**.

It is designed for small businesses and home offices. It connects work-related devices and allows resource sharing, communication and internet access.

### Internet as a Network of Networks

Individual networks connect with each other through network infrastructure and Internet Service Providers (ISPs), forming the global internet.

## 3. Mobile Devices

Mobile devices are portable computing devices that can connect to the internet.

Examples:

- Smartphones
- Tablets
- Smartwatches
- Smart glasses

They provide internet access, communication, navigation and other digital services.

## 4. Connected Home Devices

Many household devices can connect to the internet and be monitored or controlled remotely.

Examples:

- Security systems
- Smart appliances
- Smart TVs
- Gaming consoles

**Example:** A connected security camera allows users to monitor their homes remotely using a smartphone.

## 5. Other Connected Devices

### Smart Cars
Connected vehicles can access navigation, entertainment, maintenance information and emergency assistance.

### RFID Tags
RFID stands for **Radio Frequency Identification**. It uses radio waves to identify and track tagged objects.

### Sensors and Actuators

- **Sensors:** Detect environmental changes such as temperature, movement and light.
- **Actuators:** Perform physical actions based on control signals.

### Medical Devices
Connected medical devices can monitor patient information and transmit readings to healthcare systems.

## 6. Internet of Things (IoT)

The Internet of Things refers to physical devices connected to networks that can collect, exchange and sometimes act on data.

Examples include smart cameras, smart TVs, connected cars and smart appliances.

## 7. Cybersecurity Relevance

Internet-connected devices can introduce security risks.

Examples:

- Unsecured cameras can expose private video feeds.
- Weak router passwords can allow unauthorised network access.
- Vulnerable IoT devices can become entry points for attackers.

Securing connected devices is an important part of network security.

## 8. Key Terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Internet | Global collection of interconnected networks |
| LAN | Local Area Network |
| SOHO | Small Office/Home Office |
| ISP | Internet Service Provider |
| IoT | Internet of Things |
| RFID | Radio Frequency Identification |
| Sensor | Detects environmental changes |
| Actuator | Performs a physical action |

## 9. Key Takeaways

- The internet is a network of networks.
- No single individual owns the entire internet.
- Local networks connect devices within limited areas.
- SOHO networks support small offices and home offices.
- IoT connects physical devices to networks.
- Connected devices must be secured against cyber threats.
